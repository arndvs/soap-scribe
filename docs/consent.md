# Recording consent: draft wording (G-02)

**Status: draft for review. Not legal advice.** Have a California healthcare attorney (or the practice's compliance contact) review this before any real patient is recorded. Bonnie decides the final wording and process.

## Why this is needed

California's Invasion of Privacy Act makes it an offense to record a confidential communication without the consent of **all parties** to it (Penal Code § 632). A treatment-room conversation is confidential, and everyone in the room counts: the patient, and a partner, parent or child who is present. So consent has to come before recording starts, and from everyone there.

Separately, the recording is patient health information: that's why the BAAs in G-01 are required before real use. Consent to record doesn't replace those.

## 1. Intake form line (new and existing patients)

> **Visit recording for note-taking.** Dr. Fischer may record the audio of your visit with a small recorder so that your chart note can be drafted accurately. The recording is transcribed by a service under a HIPAA business associate agreement, used only to prepare your chart note, and the transcript and draft are deleted after the note is finished. Dr. Fischer reviews and signs every note. You can say no, or ask to stop recording at any point, and your care will be exactly the same.
>
> ☐ I agree to visit recording for note-taking.  ☐ I do not agree.
> Signature ________  Date ________

Existing patients sign at their next visit; until then, ask verbally (section 3) and note it.

## 2. Sign in the treatment room

> **This visit may be recorded** to help Dr. Fischer write your chart note.
> Only with your permission. Ask anytime to pause or stop.

Place it where the patient sees it before the visit starts, next to the recorder.

## 3. What Bonnie says at the start of a recorded visit

> "Is it still OK if I record today so I can get your note right? [If someone else is in the room:] And is that OK with you too?"

If anyone says no, don't record; chart as usual. In SOAP Scribe, that visit shows as **No recording** in Today and can be drafted from previous notes (G-23).

## 4. Recording the answer

Keep the consent with the patient's intake in Jane (not in SOAP Scribe). A simple chart flag or a line in the intake is enough to look it up.
